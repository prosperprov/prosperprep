# Free Grade 6 / middle-school curricula — research notes

Prefer materials we can **adapt into Prosper Prep lesson text** (with attribution), not only link out. Below is a practical shortlist for ELA, Math, Science, Social Studies (and writing).

| Source | URL | License / what we can do |
|--------|-----|---------------------------|
| **Open Up Resources 6–8 Math** (Illustrative Mathematics) | https://access.openupresources.org/curricula/our6-8math/en/grade-6/index.html | **CC BY 4.0** — adapt/remix with attribution. Best open full-course Grade 6 math (ratios, rates, percents, expressions, rational numbers, area/SA, stats). |
| **Core Knowledge Math (CKMath) 6–8** | https://www.coreknowledge.org/mathematics/ · downloads https://www.coreknowledge.org/download-free-curriculum/ | Based on IM/OUR (CC BY 4.0 lineage); Core Knowledge free download terms: use/adapt/share with attribution; **do not sell** the original or adaptations as a commercial curriculum package. |

| **CK-12 Middle School Math 6** | https://www.ck12.org/ | Free to use on-platform; **CK-12 Curriculum Materials License** is more restrictive than CC BY for wholesale reuse — prefer linking or high-level topic alignment over copying FlexBook prose. |

**Shipped approach:** original Prosper Prep lesson bodies; conceptual scope aligned with OUR/IM Grade 6 units (ratios → percents → integers → expressions/equations → area/SA → statistics).

## Writing (cross-cutting)

Use CKLA writing tasks, CER from science/literacy, and Prosper Prep argument/outline lessons already in the Grade 6 ELA set. Utah math foundations also model written mathematical explanation.

## Legal posture for Prosper Prep

1. Prefer **CC BY** (OUR/IM, Utah math) and **U.S. government public domain** (NASA, USGS, many LOC items).
2. For **Core Knowledge**, attribute and do not sell their materials or our near-copies as a commercial curriculum package.
3. For **Eureka/EngageNY** and **CK-12**, align **topics**; author **original** student-facing prose unless counsel clears a specific excerpt.
4. Always keep light “Source note” lines in lessons when we consciously align to an open scope.
